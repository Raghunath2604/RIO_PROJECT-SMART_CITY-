🎯 Research Gap Resolution Summary

Gap 1: Minority Class Starvation Solved (RQ4)

Unweighted models in IoT security literature achieve only 3–30% recall on rare attacks. Our per-class Bernoulli subsampling + balanced class-weighting boosted Web-Based Recall to 84.0% and Brute Force Recall to 73.4%.

Gap 3: True Single-Row Streaming Latency (RQ3)

Batch timing gives an illusion of low latency. Under true per-packet streaming arrival, Random Forest takes 33.8 ms/sample (unviable for edge), whereas CompactMLP consumes only 132 µs at 135 KB.
